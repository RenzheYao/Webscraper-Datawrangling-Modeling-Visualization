### **1. Executive Summary & Market Findings**

The French tea market presents a mature, segmented landscape with distinct consumer preferences and pricing dynamics across product categories. The analysis reveals that **black tea dominates** in volume (132 products), followed closely by green tea (127 products), indicating strong foundational demand for traditional, accessible tea varieties.

Key findings include:
- **Black Tea is the most dominant category**, primarily sourced from India, China/Sri Lanka blends, and Japan. India leads in volume share at 25%, while Chinese-origin black teas are highly valued but priced competitively.
- **Green tea commands premium pricing**, especially Japanese green teas and matcha. The median price for Japanese green tea is €279/kg — nearly double that of Chinese green tea — reflecting strong cultural perception, quality expectations, and perceived exclusivity.
- **Price dispersion is significant** across origins: while Indian black tea averages €183/kg, Japanese matcha averages over €1,300/kg, signaling a clear premium segment for specialty products.
- The French market shows a **clear price-performance gap**: Chinese green teas are priced modestly (mean €192.59/kg), whereas Japanese varieties command 2–4x higher prices due to brand positioning and heritage.

These dynamics suggest that **Chinese tea exporters can successfully enter the French market by strategically differentiating their offerings across price tiers**, leveraging cost advantages, quality consistency, and growing consumer interest in authentic Asian flavors — particularly in green tea segments.
